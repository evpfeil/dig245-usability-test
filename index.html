Test 1
Time: 7 Seconds
Task: Find Shopify Products

Our tester, Brooke, started off on the Shopify website. Once there, she quickly located the products tab and then the "online" tab within that. She clicked on it. Mentioned this part was was extremely easy.

Test 2
Time: 4 Minutes 3 Seconds
Task: Find the Magic Spoon Link

This took Brooke a long time, as she struggled to locate Magic Spoon on the online page. She even changed pages at one point. When she eventually returned to the online page, she scrolled past the Magic Spoon link. We noticed that the 3 columns scrolling in differnt directions made the website feel overwhelming to the user and complicated to really focus on one section. Brooke eventually felt annoyed and irritated after about 2 minutes of looking.
"I do not recommend this website to anyone" -Brooke

Test 3
Time: 4 Minutes 23 Seconds
Task: Add the Magic Crunch Cup to the cart

Brooke tried finding a search bar to look up the product, but there was none. At this point, she was already frustrated. As a result, she tried to locate the cup as quickly as possible. She rapidly clicked different tabs to see if she could find the cup, but the cup was only linked in one place: the All Products section, at the bottom of the page. This made the process extremely slow and uncomfortable.

Test 4
Time: 33 Seconds
Task: Find the amount of protein in a Magic Spoon pastry

With her prior clicking around tabs, Brooke had seen the pastry tab before. She easily found the pastry tab at the top of the screen and located the nutritional information.

Test 5
Time: 8 Seconds
Task: Locate their Instagram

Brooke easily found Magic Spoon's Instagram at the bottom of the page. This is common for websites, and Brooke seemed familiar as she started scrolling down the moment she received her instructions.
